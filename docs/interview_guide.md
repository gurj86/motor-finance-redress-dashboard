# Interview Guide

## 30-second explanation

“I created a fictional motor finance commission remediation dashboard using current FCA public information. It structures agreement, commission and disclosure evidence, identifies potential scope or exception issues, produces clearly labelled illustrative redress scenarios and converts the case results into governance information. It also reflects that parts of the scheme are currently suspended due to legal challenges, so every output requires current-rule validation and human approval.”

## Why is this useful?

Large remediation programmes need consistent data, clear evidence gaps, controlled calculations, prioritised queues and management information. The dashboard shows how case-level work can support those requirements without allowing automation to make final decisions.

## How did you use AI?

“I defined the business questions, evidence fields, remediation controls and expected outputs. AI helped convert that design into code and documentation. I then tested the logic and challenged whether the results were reasonable.”

## Why are the redress figures illustrative?

The scheme is subject to legal challenge, some provisions are suspended and real calculations require complete agreement data and the operative firm methodology. Presenting scenarios as final payments would be misleading.

## What would you do in a live project?

Validate the current rule version, obtain complete lender and broker evidence, test cohorts and exceptions, reperform redress, complete maker-checker QA, prioritise vulnerable customers, track communications and payments, and report emerging risks to governance.
