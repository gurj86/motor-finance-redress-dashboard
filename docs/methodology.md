# Methodology, Controls and Limitations

## Regulatory basis

The dashboard reflects high-level FCA information available on 17 September 2026. The FCA introduced PS26/3 in March 2026, but parts of the scheme are suspended during legal challenges. The project must therefore be read as a remediation-readiness and portfolio demonstration.

## Scope indicators represented

The fictional rules consider agreement dates, product type, DCA, high-commission indicators, contractual ties, disclosure, commission de minimis levels, no-interest agreements, high-value loan thresholds, prior resolution, business purpose and incomplete evidence.

The full FCA rules and legal interpretation are not reproduced.

## Illustrative calculation

For an indicatively eligible fictional case, the prototype:

1. Estimates loss using 21% of cost of credit for agreements before April 2014 and 17% thereafter.
2. Averages the estimated loss and commission.
3. Applies the lowest illustrative cap: 90% of commission, adjusted credit-cost cap or actual cost of credit.
4. Adds simplified compensatory interest using a fictional rate and deprivation period.

The real scheme uses more detailed rules, data and annual interest assumptions. The project does not calculate real compensation.

## Human control points

A qualified reviewer must validate source evidence, apply the current approved rules, resolve missing data, reperform the calculation, complete QA, consider vulnerability and deceased-customer handling, record rationale and obtain payment approval.

## Production controls

- Approved rule version and change governance
- Secure data access and minimisation
- Agreement-to-output evidence lineage
- Broker data request tracking
- Calculation reconciliation and maker-checker QA
- Cohort and exception validation
- Vulnerability and accessibility controls
- Duplicate-representative resolution
- Scam prevention and verified payment details
- Audit trail and regulatory reporting
