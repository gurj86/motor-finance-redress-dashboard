# Example AI Prompts — Motor Finance Remediation

## Agreement and evidence review

> Review the fictional motor finance agreement and identify the agreement date, product type, amount financed, total cost of credit, APR, commission amount, commission arrangement, disclosure evidence, broker/lender relationship, complaint status and any prior resolution. Cite the source for every material finding. Separate confirmed facts, inferences and missing evidence. Do not decide eligibility or calculate a payment where evidence is incomplete.

## Commission-arrangement challenge

> Test whether the evidence indicates a discretionary commission arrangement, a high commission arrangement or a contractual tie. Check the relevant thresholds and exceptions separately. Do not infer non-disclosure merely because a commission was paid. Identify exactly what evidence supports the proposed classification and what remains uncertain.

## Redress quality assurance

> Audit the illustrative calculation. Reperform each input and step, identify the rule version used, test all relevant caps, confirm the interest assumption, and compare the result with the source agreement data. Flag any missing payment schedule, commission evidence, APR benchmark or date information. Do not approve redress or create a payment instruction.

## Governance reporting

> Aggregate the reviewed fictional cases by indicative status, commission arrangement, exclusion reason, evidence gap, vulnerability, age, redress band and QA result. Identify recurring patterns without claiming causation. State the population size, limitations, legal-challenge dependency, priority actions and accountable owner questions.

## Challenge an AI conclusion

> Review the draft AI conclusion and identify any statement that lacks evidence, applies the wrong date or threshold, overlooks an exception, treats missing evidence as a negative result, or presents an illustrative calculation as final compensation. Return the unsupported statement, the issue, required evidence and corrected wording.
